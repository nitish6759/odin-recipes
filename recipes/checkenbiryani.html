Discription

Chicken Biryani is a delicious and flavorful rice dish made with tender chicken, basmati rice, aromatic spices, onions, and fresh herbs. The combination of spices and fragrant rice gives the biryani a rich taste and wonderful aroma.

Chicken Biryani is a popular dish enjoyed on special occasions as well as regular meals. The chicken is cooked with spices and then layered with partially cooked basmati rice, allowing all the flavors to blend together while the rice becomes soft and aromatic.

Ingradients

  • rice
  • water
  • chicken
  • biryani masala

Steps

  1. put rice and chicken in pot
  2. add biryani masala
  3. add water
  4. close
